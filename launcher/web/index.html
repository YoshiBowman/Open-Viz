How to get an MVR file out of grandMA3 or Vectorworks

Their own show files (.show, .vwx) are closed formats that cannot be read here. Both export the rig as MVR, an open format that carries the fixtures, their positions, their patch and the fixture types.

grandMA3: Menu, then Patch. Press Export at the bottom, leave the file type on MVR and press Export. The file lands in grandMA3's gma3_library/mvr folder, and is offered below once it is there.

Vectorworks Spotlight: File, Export, Export MVR.

Log in to GDTF Share

Sent only to gdtf-share.com. With Stay logged in ticked, the password is kept in the macOS Keychain, where your other passwords are, and Openviz logs in by itself from then on. Without it, nothing is saved and you stay logged in until Openviz quits. No account yet? Sign up free at gdtf-share.com.
